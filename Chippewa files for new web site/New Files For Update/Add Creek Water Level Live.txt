CHIPPEWA CREEK WATER LEVEL

Click this link https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-03116196 to check the Gage Height of the creek.  This measurement is taken about 4 miles north of the range but is a good indication of what is happening.  Creek can rise quickly during heavy rainfall.  A measured gage height above 16.5 feet indicates that the creek will imminently overflow its bank and begin flooding the range.  If currently at the range with water levels approaching 16.0 feet, depart immediately.
